🛠️ Methodology

The proliferation of agentic AI tools has sparked new ways for humans to express our creativity. Creatrweb is both a service and framework for creative people who want to leverage AI tools to enhance, not lose, their creativity and critical thinking. While I utilize AI tools to enhance my own creativity, I place the vision front-and-center to make sure that I'm behind the wheel, rather than the other way around. Thanks to my developed taste, style, and modes of thought, I understand how to implement my vision using AI tools, and I can help you do the same.

Fun fact: I also hold a Master's degree (MSc equivalent) in Data Science and I'm in training in a Master's program in Software Engineering, so I understand the underlying technology and can help you make sense of it as well.
